ComposeFootballPitch

A Compose Multiplatform library for rendering customizable football pitches and team lineups on Android and Desktop (iOS via KMP source).

Designed for sports apps, live score dashboards, analysis tools, and anyone who wants a FIFA-style football pitch powered by Jetpack Compose / Compose Multiplatform.
